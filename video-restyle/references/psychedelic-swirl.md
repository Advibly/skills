# Template: psychedelic-swirl

Late-60s psychedelia in motion. The whole frame melts into liquid marbled
color: the background is a constantly flowing swirl of saturated rainbow
pigment, and the person is repainted in posterized high-contrast tones with
flowing painterly edges. Groovy, hypnotic, maximal. The one template whose
background never stops moving by definition.

**Pick it when**: the content is creative, musical, provocative, or
counter-cultural (art drops, music promo, bold opinions, brand moments that
want to feel trippy) and the user wants maximum visual heat with a painted
subject.

**Subject treatment: full repaint.** The person becomes a psychedelic
poster painting OF THE SAME person. Replace the identity block's opening
with this variant:

```
Transform this clip into a psychedelic art film of the exact same scene.
The person becomes a posterized psychedelic painting of themselves: the
same recognizable face, the same identity, the same expressions, the same
mouth movements and lip-sync, the same hairstyle and clothing, matching the
source frame for frame. Their face stays crisp and readable inside the
posterized color. Their spoken performance continues unchanged. Foreground
props they hold or touch stay present, painted in the same style.
```

## Style block (verbatim in every segment prompt)

```
Style: a 1960s psychedelic poster brought to life. The background is a
liquid marbled swirl of saturated colors flowing slowly and continuously,
like wet ink folding into itself. The person is posterized into bold flat
color zones with smooth flowing painterly edges, rim-lit by the swirl
behind them. Line work bends and flows, nothing is straight. Colors are
maximally saturated and never muddy. The whole frame reads as one hypnotic
living poster, always gently in motion.
```

## Variation axes (rotate per segment)

**Axis 1: the swirl pattern.** Rotate so no two adjacent segments repeat.

1. **Liquid marble**: wet-ink marbling folding into itself edge to edge.
2. **Concentric rings**: rings of alternating color radiating outward from
   behind the subject's head, breathing slowly.
3. **Melting waves**: vertical taffy-pull waves dripping downward in slow
   motion.
4. **Kaleidoscope bloom**: a symmetric mandala pattern unfolding petal by
   petal behind the subject.

**Axis 2: the palette.** One saturated trio per segment, never repeated on
adjacent segments: magenta + orange + teal, purple + acid green + gold,
hot pink + cyan + lemon, crimson + violet + amber.

**Axis 3: groovy motifs.** On segments with concrete `topics`, one motif in
the flowing poster style drifts in the background: a blooming flower, an
open eye, a radiant sun, a winding star trail. One per segment maximum,
kept behind the subject.

## Energy moves

The energy is liquid, not cut-based. No hard cuts inside a segment; the
swirl accelerates, blooms, and inverts on words instead:

- **Color inversion flash**: exactly on the strongest emphasis word, the
  palette inverts for a single beat then flows back. The loudest move; use
  it once or twice per video.
- **Swirl surge**: on an emphasis word, the background flow visibly speeds
  up and swells, then relaxes.
- **Palette melt**: on a phrase turn, the current trio melts into the next
  trio, colors bleeding through the swirl.

## Beat vocabulary (anchor these to words)

- **Bloom open**: on the opening word, the swirl blooms outward from behind
  the subject and settles into its flow.
- **Motif drift-in**: exactly on the word that names a topic, the groovy
  motif flowers into view in the background.
- **Surge**: on a mid-segment emphasis word, the swirl surge.
- **Inversion**: on the segment's peak word, the color inversion flash.
- **Slow settle**: on the final word, the flow eases to its slowest drift
  for the cut.

A packed 8-10s segment carries two or three of these; the peak word gets the
inversion or the surge.

## Caption treatment

Captions are optional and off by default; groovy lettering is a spelling
risk. When the user wants them, keep 3-4 short words:

```
A short caption reads exactly "TUNE IN NOW", spelled exactly, every word
correct, in rounded groovy bubble lettering, curved gently along the lower
third, in the palette's lightest color with a dark outline.
```

Verify spelling extra carefully on this template; re-roll on any doubt.

## Example beat timeline

Segment spoken line: "Your feed is full of the same thing. This is
something else entirely." (9s, emphasis on "else")

```
The segment runs this beat timeline over the spoken words:
- It opens on the word "Your" as a magenta, orange and teal liquid marble
  blooms outward from behind the posterized person and settles into a slow
  continuous fold.
- Exactly on the word "same" the swirl visibly surges, swelling and
  speeding for a moment before relaxing.
- Exactly on the word "else" the palette inverts for a single beat then
  flows back, and the flow eases to its slowest drift as the segment ends.
  The person's voice and the room's sound continue naturally.
```

## Failure modes

- **The face melted or went generic**: the swirl ate the subject. Reinforce
  "their face stays crisp and readable inside the posterized color" right
  after the identity lines, simplify axis 1, re-roll.
- **Lip-sync smeared**: add "the mouth shapes stay crisp and readable
  through the posterization" and re-roll; this template fights small mouth
  shapes hardest.
- **Colors turned muddy or brown**: too many hues named. Hold each segment
  to its three palette colors, restate "maximally saturated and never
  muddy".
- **The swirl froze**: the flow lines got trimmed. Restore "flowing slowly
  and continuously" and name the motion (folding, breathing, dripping,
  unfolding).
- **Caption illegible or misspelled**: drop to 3 words or drop the caption;
  this template sells without text.
- **Strobe or seizure-fast flicker appeared**: say "the flow is slow and
  smooth, no flicker, no strobing" and re-roll.
