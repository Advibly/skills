# Template: neon-vaporwave

The subject glows inside a retro-future synthwave world: deep gradient
skies, a neon grid horizon, chrome and neon typography, rim light in pink
and cyan. Night-drive energy. Where podcast-pop is daylight loud, this is
midnight loud.

**Pick it when**: the brand or audience runs on gaming, music, fitness,
crypto, nightlife, streetwear, or the user asks for synthwave, retrowave,
neon, or "aesthetic" looks.

**Subject treatment: cutout-real with a glow.** The person stays photoreal
but takes scene lighting: neon rim light and a soft glow edge instead of the
sticker outline. Standard identity block from SKILL.md.

## Style block (verbatim in every segment prompt)

```
Style: a retro-future synthwave world. The person stays photoreal, lit by
the scene: a hot pink rim light on one side, an electric cyan rim light on
the other, a soft neon glow tracing their silhouette. Behind them a deep
gradient night sky and a glowing neon wireframe grid receding to the
horizon. Neon sign elements and soft lens bloom live in the background.
Blacks are deep, neons are saturated, the frame has a subtle VHS-era
softness. The background pulses and drifts slowly like a synth track's
rhythm. The whole frame reads as one continuous neon night scene.
```

## Variation axes (rotate per segment)

**Axis 1: the backdrop.** Rotate: the classic grid-to-horizon with a
setting striped sun, a neon city skyline silhouette, a starfield with
drifting chrome shapes (sphere, pyramid, torus), a wall of glowing neon
sign tubes, a dark tunnel of light streaks racing past.

**Axis 2: the neon pair.** Two neon hues per segment: pink + cyan, purple +
teal, magenta + electric blue, orange + violet, green + pink. The rim light
follows the pair. Adjacent segments differ.

**Axis 3: neon props.** One per segment when `topics` are concrete: a neon
outline icon of the topic hovering beside the subject (a neon brain, a neon
controller, a neon dollar sign), a chrome 3D shape drifting, or a glowing
wireframe frame around the caption.

## Energy moves

- "Exactly on the emphasis word the neon lights flare brighter for a beat and
  settle."
- One segment may take "light streaks race past, like the camera is moving
  through the scene" for velocity.
- One punch-in per video works ("exactly on the word X a tighter crop, face
  half-lit pink, half-lit cyan").

## Beat vocabulary (anchor these to words)

The neon world pulses on the spoken words:

- **Neon prop pop**: exactly on the word that names a topic, a neon outline icon
  glows in beside the shoulder.
- **Backdrop swap**: on a phrase turn, the backdrop cuts to the next look in the
  rotation with the next neon pair.
- **Flare**: on the emphasis word, the neon lights flare brighter and settle.
- **Light-streak rush**: on a momentum word, streaks race past for velocity.
- **Caption glow-in**: the chrome-and-neon caption flickers on and holds on its
  spoken beat.

A packed 8-10s segment uses two or three; the emphasis word gets the flare or
the punch-in.

## Caption treatment

```
A neon caption reads exactly "LEVEL UP TONIGHT", spelled exactly, every
word correct, glowing chrome-and-neon uppercase letters with a subtle
flicker, anchored lower third.
```

## Example beat timeline

Segment spoken line: "This is your night to play like you actually mean it."
(8s, emphasis on "mean it")

```
The segment runs this beat timeline over the spoken words:
- It opens on the word "This" over a neon city skyline silhouette under a deep
  gradient sky, drifting slowly; neon pair purple and teal, rim light following.
  Exactly on the word "play" a neon outline controller icon glows in beside the
  person's shoulder.
- Exactly on the word "night" light streaks race past for a beat, like the
  camera moving through the scene.
- Exactly on the words "mean it" the neon lights flare brighter and settle, and
  a neon caption flickers on reading exactly "PLAY LIKE YOU MEAN IT", spelled
  exactly, every word correct, glowing chrome-and-neon uppercase, lower third.
  The person's voice continues naturally.
```

## Failure modes

- **The person turned CGI**: keep "the person stays photoreal, lit by the
  scene" intact and adjacent to the identity lines.
- **Neon soup** (everything glows, nothing reads): limit to the two named
  hues plus deep blacks; cut one background element.
- **The face is underlit / silhouetted**: add "the face stays clearly lit
  and readable, key light on the face".
- **Flicker went strobe**: "subtle flicker" and "pulses slowly"; strobing
  reads as a defect, re-roll.
- **Grid horizon bends or melts**: add "the wireframe grid stays straight
  and stable, receding evenly".
- **The scene sits static**: the beats were dropped. Restore 2-3 beats (prop
  pop, flare, light-streak rush, caption glow-in) anchored to exact words; pack
  the segment to 8-10s.
