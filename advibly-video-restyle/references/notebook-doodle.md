# Template: notebook-doodle

The subject sits photoreal on a page of notebook paper while a hand
annotates the world around them live: ink doodles, arrows, underlines,
highlighter swipes, sticky notes. Casual, smart, teacher-energy. The
explainer template: it makes talking feel like learning.

**Pick it when**: the content teaches or breaks something down (how-tos,
tips, "3 things I learned", tutorials, coaching) and the user wants
approachable rather than loud.

**Subject treatment: cutout-real.** The person stays fully photoreal,
cut onto the page. Standard identity block from SKILL.md.

## Style block (verbatim in every segment prompt)

```
Style: the person is cut out photoreal and placed on a sheet of notebook
paper that fills the frame: ruled lines, a red margin line, slight paper
texture, soft natural shadow under the cutout. Around them, hand-drawn
annotations live on the page: black ballpoint ink doodles, arrows,
underlines, circled words, plus one highlighter color used sparingly. The
annotations wobble slightly like a boiling hand-drawn animation. Handwritten
caption words sit on the ruled lines. The whole frame reads as a smart,
casual annotated notebook page in motion.
```

## Variation axes (rotate per segment)

**Axis 1: the paper.** Rotate: ruled notebook page, graph/grid paper, a
yellow legal pad, a dotted bullet-journal page, a torn-out page taped onto a
desk surface.

**Axis 2: the highlighter color.** One per segment: yellow, pink, green,
blue, orange. Adjacent segments differ.

**Axis 3: the annotations.** Driven by the segment's `topics`: a doodled
icon of the topic (a lightbulb, a rising chart, a coffee cup, a brain) plus
one or two functional marks (an arrow pointing at the subject when they
gesture, a circled key word, a numbered list tick when the speech
enumerates). Two or three marks per segment, never a full page of clutter.

**Axis 4: sticky notes.** At most one per segment, only when a short side
note earns it (a number, a "!!", a tiny keyword). Rotate its color with the
highlighter.

## Energy moves

- "Exactly on the emphasis word it gets underlined twice in ink and swiped
  with the highlighter."
- One segment may take a punch-in ("exactly on the word X the framing crops
  tighter, like leaning into the page").
- Enumerations in speech are gold here: number the page (1., 2., 3.) as each
  number is spoken, across consecutive segments; continuity across cuts makes
  the video feel designed.

## Beat vocabulary (anchor these to words)

The page is annotated live, a hand drawing on the spoken words:

- **Icon draw-in**: exactly on the word that names a topic, a doodled icon
  sketches itself beside the head.
- **Arrow / circle**: on a gesture or key noun, an ink arrow points to it or a
  circle draws around it.
- **List tick**: as each number or list item is spoken, a "1.", "2." writes in.
- **Underline + highlight**: on the emphasis word, the double underline and
  highlighter swipe (above).
- **Caption write-on**: the handwritten caption writes itself onto the ruled
  line on its spoken beat.

A packed 8-10s segment uses two or three marks total (never a cluttered page),
each landing on its word.

## Caption treatment

```
A handwritten caption reads exactly "START BEFORE YOU'RE READY", spelled
exactly, every word correct, neat black ink handwriting sitting on the
ruled lines in the lower third, with a yellow highlighter swipe behind it.
```

## Example beat timeline

Segment spoken line: "Ideas are cheap. What matters is doing the boring work."
(8s, emphasis on "work")

```
The segment runs this beat timeline over the spoken words:
- It opens on the word "Ideas" over graph paper with a green highlighter accent;
  exactly on "Ideas" a doodled lightbulb icon sketches itself in beside the
  person's head, and an ink arrow draws from it to their raised hand.
- Exactly on the word "boring" a small sticky note reading "!!" pops onto the
  page.
- Exactly on the word "work" it is underlined twice in ink and swiped with the
  green highlighter, and the handwritten caption finishes writing on the ruled
  line reading exactly "DO THE BORING WORK", spelled exactly, every word
  correct, neat black ink handwriting, lower third. The person's voice
  continues naturally.
```

## Failure modes

- **Handwriting captions misspell** (the most common failure here):
  re-roll first; if it repeats, shorten to 3-4 words or switch the caption
  line to "clean printed marker capitals" which the model spells more
  reliably than cursive.
- **Page cluttered with marks**: cap at "two or three ink marks total" in
  the beat timeline.
- **The subject got sketched**: keep "cut out photoreal" adjacent to the
  person lines; the paper world takes the style, the person does not.
- **Annotations frozen**: restore the "wobble slightly like a boiling
  hand-drawn animation" line.
- **Highlighter flooded the page**: "one highlighter swipe" and name where.
- **Nothing draws on the words**: the beats were dropped. Restore 2-3 marks
  (icon draw-in, arrow, underline+highlight, caption write-on) anchored to
  exact words; pack the segment to 8-10s.
