# Template: watercolor-wash

The whole frame becomes a living watercolor painting: the person, their
clothes, the room, everything is repainted in translucent pigment washes on
textured cold-press paper, while the performance and lip-sync carry through.
Soft, warm, editorial. Where podcast-pop shouts, watercolor-wash breathes.

**Pick it when**: the content is calm, personal, premium, or story-driven
(founder letters, wellness, food, travel, education) and the user wants an
artful transformation rather than retention energy.

**Subject treatment: full repaint.** The person becomes a watercolor
painting OF THE SAME person. Replace the identity block's opening with this
variant:

```
Transform this clip into a watercolor painting of the exact same scene. The
person becomes a hand-painted watercolor version of themselves: the same
recognizable face, the same identity, the same expressions, the same mouth
movements and lip-sync, the same hairstyle and clothing, matching the source
frame for frame. Their spoken performance continues unchanged. Foreground
props they hold or touch stay present, painted in the same style.
```

## Style block (verbatim in every segment prompt)

```
Style: a hand-painted watercolor film on cold-press paper. Translucent
pigment washes with visible paper grain showing through, soft wet-on-wet
edges where colors meet, deliberate loose brushwork, thin ink linework
holding the forms, small white paper reserves left unpainted as highlights.
Colors bloom gently at the edges as things move. The background is
simplified into broad soft washes rather than detail. The whole frame reads
as one continuous living painting, warm and editorial.
```

## Variation axes (rotate per segment)

**Axis 1: the wash palette.** One dominant temperature per segment, rotated:
warm ochre + burnt sienna, cool cerulean + payne's grey, botanical greens +
cream, dusk violet + rose, sun yellow + soft coral, ink blue + warm grey.
Adjacent segments never share a temperature.

**Axis 2: the background wash.** What the simplified background becomes:
broad horizontal washes like a sky, a loose wet bloom radiating from behind
the subject, faint painted hints of the original room, scattered pigment
spatter and granulation, a plain paper field with a single tinted wash.

**Axis 3: painted accents.** On segments with concrete `topics`, one or two
small loose watercolor motifs float in the background (a painted leaf, a tiny
boat, a coffee cup, an arrow). Keep them background elements; the face stays
the subject.

## Energy moves

Watercolor's energy is chromatic, not kinetic, but it still moves on words.
No punch-ins; the medium reads best at a steady framing. Its beats are gentle
and still anchor to exact spoken words:

- **Bloom**: exactly on the emphasis word, "the wash behind the person blooms
  outward in a deeper pigment". The strongest such bloom is the segment's peak.
- **Palette bleed**: on a phrase turn, "the wash shifts from <this palette> to
  <the next palette> bleeding across the paper".
- **Accent draw-in**: exactly on the word that names a topic, "a small loose
  watercolor <motif> paints itself into the corner".
- **Settle**: on the final word, "the pigment settles and the paper stills".

A packed 8-10s segment carries two or three of these soft beats so the frame
is a living painting, not a static filter.

## Caption treatment

Captions are optional in this template and off by default; the look sells
itself. When the user wants them:

```
A short caption reads exactly "SLOW DOWN TO GROW", spelled exactly, every
word correct, hand-lettered in dark ink brush calligraphy near the lower
third, sitting on the paper like part of the painting.
```

## Example beat timeline

Segment spoken line: "The best ideas come when you slow down and let them
breathe." (9s, emphasis on "breathe")

```
The segment runs this beat timeline over the spoken words:
- It opens on the word "The" in cool cerulean and payne's grey washes, the
  background a loose wet bloom of blue radiating from behind the person,
  granulating into the paper grain. Exactly on the word "ideas" a small loose
  watercolor paper boat paints itself into the upper corner.
- Exactly on the word "slow" the wash shifts, botanical greens and cream
  bleeding softly across the paper.
- Exactly on the word "breathe" the wash behind the person blooms outward in a
  deeper pigment, then settles and the paper stills. The person's voice and the
  room's sound continue naturally.
```

## Failure modes

- **The face went generic** (a painted stranger): reinforce "the same
  recognizable face, a watercolor portrait of this exact person" and re-roll.
  If it persists, reduce axis-2 complexity; busy backgrounds steal capacity
  from the face.
- **Output looks like a photo with a filter**: the paper lines got trimmed.
  Restore "visible paper grain", "white paper reserves", "loose brushwork".
- **Colors turned muddy**: too many pigments named. Keep each segment to the
  two palette pigments plus ink.
- **Lip-sync smeared**: watercolor softness fights small mouth shapes.
  Re-roll first; if it persists, add "the mouth shapes stay crisp and
  readable through the paint".
- **The painting sits static**: the beats were dropped. Restore two or three
  soft beats (bloom, palette bleed, accent draw-in) anchored to exact words;
  the calm look still needs pigment moving on the page.
